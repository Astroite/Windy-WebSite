实时天气Live weather

天气是真的，所以访客也是 The weather is real — so are the visitors

壁纸读取你所在区域的真实气象快照：风场、降雨、温度、台风。天象访客的出现条件与这份数据绑定—— 下雨时才有雨带与雷雨云，强风区才起风线，雨转晴的瞬间才可能挂起彩虹。 The wallpaper reads a real weather snapshot for your region — wind, rain, temperature, cyclones. Each visitor is bound to that data: showers and thunderclouds appear only in rain, wind streaks in gales, and a rainbow only in the moment rain turns to clear.

  • 数据来源 Open-Meteo / NOAA GFS，台风来自 GDACS Data from Open-Meteo / NOAA GFS; cyclones from GDACS
  • 局部降雨、强风、雨区边界都会影响访客在何处现身 Local rain, wind and rain-edge zones decide where a visitor shows up
  • 断网也不崩：地图与城市照常渲染，缓存数据带「旧数据」标记自动重试 Offline-safe: map and cities still render; cached data is marked "stale" and retried
