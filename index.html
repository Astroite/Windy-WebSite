真实天气底座Built on real weather

天气不是装饰，而是每条玩法的共同语言 Weather is not decoration — it is the language every system shares

Windborne 把风、雨、温度、昼夜和风暴整理成同一份天气状态。它同时改变地图氛围、访客机会与牧场住客的表现。 Windborne turns wind, rain, temperature, daylight and storms into one shared weather state. It shapes the map atmosphere, visitor opportunities and ranch reactions together.

  • 局部降雨、强风和雨区边界会决定访客能否出现、从哪里进入 Local rain, strong wind and rain edges decide whether and where visitors appear
  • 昼夜、晨昏与风暴氛围缓慢过渡，不伪造没有数据支撑的天气 Day, twilight and storm atmosphere transition gently without inventing unsupported weather
  • 实时数据暂不可用时保留最近画面并自动重试；缓存不会产生新的正式收藏 When live data is unavailable, the last scene remains and retries; cached weather grants no new collection progress

帮一位住客，在合适的栖息地安个家 Help a resident find a home in the right habitat

从已经遇见的住客里挑一位，拖到地图上的天空、水域或陆地。一放手就会保存，真实天气还会改变它的动作与状态。 Choose a resident you have met and place it in the sky, water or land on your map. Placement saves immediately, while real weather changes its motion and state.

  • 天空、水域、陆地三条独立轨道，各有两位初始伙伴 Sky, water and land have separate tracks, each starting with two companions
  • 住客安顿满一小时后正式定居，并沿栖息地轨道带来下一位伙伴 After one hour in place, a resident settles and unlocks the next companion on that habitat track
  • 撤回只改变当前布局，已经完成的定居与解锁不会丢失 Withdrawing changes the layout only; completed settlements and unlocks remain
